GOOD TO KNOW

A few things
you might be wondering.

Explore some common questions about working with our photography and film studio.

How early should we enquire?

We recommend enquiring as early as possible, especially for weddings and destination events. Availability depends on your event date and the studio's existing commitments.

Does submitting an enquiry confirm my date?

No. An enquiry helps us understand your plans. Your date is not reserved until availability, the proposal and the booking terms have been agreed and completed.

Can we discuss our budget before choosing a collection?

Absolutely. Share your requirements and approximate budget in the enquiry. We can discuss suitable options before you decide.

Can we book photography and films together?

Yes. You can enquire about photography, films or a combination of both. We'll discuss the scope and suitable coverage for your event.

Do you undertake destination events?

You can enquire about events at different destinations. Include the location and date in your enquiry so that we can discuss travel arrangements and availability.

Can we share photography references or a mood board?

Yes. References help us understand the visual direction you like. We can discuss how to adapt those ideas into a creative approach that suits your celebration.

Will you help us plan the photography schedule?

We can discuss the photography and film requirements with you and coordinate the agreed coverage around your event schedule.

What if our event schedule changes?

Let the studio know about changes as soon as possible. We can discuss their impact based on the agreed coverage and booking terms.

Can our family share important moments to capture?

Certainly. Share important people, traditions and moments during planning. This helps us understand what is especially meaningful to you.

When will we receive our photographs and films?

Delivery timelines depend on the project and are discussed in your proposal or booking agreement. We'll communicate the agreed timeline before the event.

How will our final work be delivered?

The delivery method and included formats will be explained in your proposal. Ask us about your preferred way to access and share your photographs and films.

Can we request additional edits or albums?

You can discuss additional edits, albums and other deliverables with us. Their availability and any additional costs will be clarified before approval.
